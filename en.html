THE PROBLEM

Two groups who need each other, and no market between them

In Baku you can have a restaurant meal delivered in thirty minutes. You cannot buy the dolma someone's mother made this morning — the food most people in this city would actually rather eat.

On the other side of that gap are thousands of people, most of them women, who cook to a professional standard every single day and have never earned a manat from it. Not because the skill is missing. Because no one built the market.

Restaurant delivery cannot serve this

Wolt, Bolt Food and Yango are built on restaurant partners — commercial kitchens, commercial licences, commercial menus. A home kitchen does not fit that model, and adapting to it would mean rebuilding the business they already have.

The work is invisible, so it is unpaid

A woman who has cooked for her family for twenty-eight years has a skill worth money and no way to sell it. Duemo does not ask her to learn a new trade or pass an interview. She cooks what she already cooks, for people who want it.

The category does not exist here yet

We reviewed thirteen platforms across the US, India, Russia and Europe. Home-cook marketplaces work elsewhere. In Azerbaijan, the Caucasus and Central Asia, nobody is running one.

HOW IT WORKS

Order to doorstep, four steps

  1. 1

    The cook lists what she is making

    She sets the portions, the price and how much notice she needs. Nothing goes live until her kitchen has been inspected and the dish tasted.

  2. 2

    The customer orders and adds a note

    Less salt, no onion, extra bread. A restaurant kitchen cooking two hundred covers cannot do this. Someone cooking your dish after you asked for it can.

  3. 3

    She cooks it after the order

    Nothing is made in advance and nothing sits under a lamp. This is why notice periods exist and why we show them before anything goes in a basket.

  4. 4

    Our courier collects and delivers

    Our own riders, in Duemo packaging, on a route the system builds. The customer follows it in the app.

WHY NOW

Nobody else is doing this, and the habit is already there

Nobody is competing for it

There is no home-cook marketplace in Azerbaijan, Georgia or Armenia. The platforms that exist sell restaurant food and will keep selling restaurant food. We are not taking share from anyone — there is nobody to take it from yet.

The behaviour is already there

Nobody in Azerbaijan needs persuading that home food is better; it is simply what people eat. Neighbours already sell it to each other quietly. What has been missing is a way to buy it from someone you do not know, pay safely, and have it arrive hot.

The model travels

Tbilisi, Yerevan, Tashkent, Almaty: same food culture, same phones, same absence of anyone doing this. Baku is where we start because we are from here, not because it is the only one that works.
